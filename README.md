# THREAD — Personal Continuity AI

> Knot your loose ends.

THREAD is a Personal Continuity AI designed to remember the things that normally fall through the gaps between messages, screenshots, documents, conversations and everyday life.

Traditional calendars remember events you manually create.

THREAD understands what happened, what it created, what is still unresolved, and what needs to happen next.

## The Problem

Our lives are scattered across chats, screenshots, emails, documents and conversations.

Someone says:
“I'll send you the PPT by October 2.”

A calendar doesn't know that a commitment was created.

THREAD does.

## How THREAD Works

DROP → UNDERSTAND → CREATE KNOT → CONNECT CONTEXT → MONITOR → RESOLVE

A user can provide:

- Text or messages
- Chat screenshots
- Documents
- Receipts
- Voice input

THREAD analyzes the information and identifies unresolved actions called **Knots**.

## Core Features

### Knots
Tracks unresolved commitments, follow-ups, borrowed items, deadlines and other loose ends.

### Threads
Connects related Knots so users can understand the complete story instead of isolated reminders.

### Waiting Room
Shows everything the user is currently waiting for from other people.

### People
Builds context around people involved in the user's Knots and commitments.

### Life Graph
Connects people, commitments, evidence and personal information into a contextual graph.

### What Am I Forgetting?
THREAD reasons across open Knots and surfaces things that may need the user's attention.

### Brief Me
Creates contextual briefs about today, work/college or a particular person.

### Before
Looks ahead at upcoming commitments and possible problems instead of only reacting after something becomes overdue.

### Evidence & Resolution
New information can resolve an existing Knot.

For example:

“Sid will send the PPT by October 2.”
→ THREAD creates a Knot.

Later:

“Sid sent the PPT.”
→ THREAD connects the new evidence and resolves the existing Knot.

## AI Capabilities

THREAD uses AI for:

- Multimodal understanding
- Screenshot interpretation
- Commitment extraction
- Person and context extraction
- Deadline detection
- Entity linking
- Resolution detection
- Contextual reasoning
- Voice-to-task processing

## What Makes THREAD Different?

THREAD is not another calendar or to-do list.

Calendars store events.

To-do apps store tasks.

THREAD stores **unfinished context**.

It remembers why something matters, who is involved, what evidence created it, what is still pending and what information can close it.
